# Pride Furniture

A big furniture mod for **Minecraft 1.12.2** (Forge / Cleanroom) — and the furniture actually **works**.
Every piece is modelled in **Blockbench**, all original art. Made for the Pride modpack, free for everyone.

## What's in it (100+ pieces and growing)
- **Kitchen** — base cabinets, drawers, sink cabinets, wall cabinets, glass cabinets and pantries in 5 finishes
  (white shaker, oak, walnut, sage, navy). **Countertops are separate**: put a cabinet down, then right-click it
  with a countertop (granite, marble, butcher block, quartz, black, concrete). Cabinets are real storage and work
  with hoppers/pipes.
- **Fridges, cheap to fancy** — basic, bottom-freezer, side-by-side, retro 50s, mini, wine, glass-door drinks
  fridge, and a **smart fridge with a screen that plays GIFs** (drop your own into `config/pridefurniture/screens/`).
  Freezers turn water buckets into ice.
- **Appliances, each with its own job** — stove/oven (cooks raw food, 4 at once), microwave, toaster (bread → toast),
  coffee maker (cocoa + water → coffee: Speed + Haste), blender (fruit → smoothie), kettle (water → tea),
  dishwasher (fills bottles, washes dye out), **trash compactor** (crushes items into Trash Cubes), trash can,
  range hood with a light.
- **Bathroom** — toilet (sit, go, stand up to flush), shower (turn it on and stand in it to get clean), bathtub
  (fill it and soak to heal), vanities, mirror, medicine cabinet, towel rack, hamper, washer, dryer, scale.
- **Needs** (optional) — bladder, bowel and hygiene meters with small on-screen bars; the bathroom fixes them.
- **Living room & office** — sofas and armchairs you can sit on, glass coffee table, TV and gaming PC with GIF
  screens, bookshelf (powers enchanting), lamps that light up, fireplace, rug, plants, wall clock, **desk globe**
  (spin it!), computer desk, office chair, **fish tank**.
- **Spiral staircases** (oak, walnut, wrought iron, white & gold) — walkable steps; stack them and each block turns
  to continue the spiral.
- **Furniture Workbench** — every piece is made here, in a catalog with tabs, search, a 3D preview and
  have/need ingredient counts.

## Download
See **[Releases](../../releases)**. Drop the jar into a 1.12.2 Forge or Cleanroom `mods/` folder.

## How it's made
Each model is described as a list of boxes (`tools/specs.py`), built and painted in Blockbench by a small helper,
and exported straight into the mod. Code is MIT — see `LICENSE`. Made with help from Claude (Anthropic's AI).
